# KewaFlow

> **"AI generates. The Admin approves. KewaFlow publishes. Results guide growth."**

Approval-first AI marketing and client-acquisition platform built for **Kewakibt Software Solutions**.

## Overview
KewaFlow gives Kewakibt a reliable marketing operating system that converts verified expertise into consistent, approved communication and measurable sales opportunities.

- **Zero Autonomous Publishing or Spending**: Every post, creative version, and ad spend action requires explicit Admin review and approval.
- **Brand Grounding**: AI generation is grounded exclusively in verified services and brand profiles.
- **Multi-Axis State Machine**: Content items track Review State, Schedule State, and Publication Outcome independently.
- **Attribution & CRM Integrity**: Organic reach and impressions are never labeled as leads; direct and assisted inquiries are cleanly tracked.
